Frequently Asked Questions

A few common questions about daycare, boarding, visits, and the meet and greet process at Pawsome Care.

Do new clients need a meet and greet first?

Yes. A meet and greet helps confirm fit, review routines, and make the first stay feel smoother for everyone.

What vaccinations do you require?

Dogs should be up to date on core vaccinations. You can share current vaccination details in the meet and greet form.

How long are dog walks and drop-in visits?

Standard bookings are 30 minutes. Extended 60-minute dog walks and drop-in visits are also available.

Do you offer holiday pricing?

Yes. Holiday periods have adjusted rates because of higher demand. Exact dates and holiday pricing are listed on the Services page.

Will I get updates while my dog is in care?

Yes. Pawsome Care focuses on clear communication, including regular photo and message updates when appropriate.

Where are you located?

Pawsome Care is based in Davis, California and provides a calm, home-based pet care environment.
