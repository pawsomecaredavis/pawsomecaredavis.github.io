Services

Flexible pet care for busy Davis pet parents

Every service is designed around safety, communication, and a calmer experience for your pet in Davis, CA. Pricing below is set about 5% below current Rover pricing where available.

Dog Daycare

$44/day

Structured daytime care in a home setting with potty breaks, supervision, play, rest time, and photo updates throughout the day.

Dog Boarding

$67/night

Overnight care for dogs who do best with a personal home environment, regular routines, and close attention instead of a crowded kennel setup.

Dog Walking

Starting at $25/walk

Reliable neighborhood walks focused on exercise, enrichment, and consistency for dogs who need a midday break or extra movement.

Pet Drop-In

$25/visit

House Sitting

$100/night
