Our Mission

Personal, home-based care with a calm routine

Pawsome Care is built around small-group, attentive pet care in Davis, CA. The goal is to give dogs a comfortable environment, clear communication with pet parents, and a more personal experience than a large, high-volume facility.

Home-Based Setting

Dogs enjoy a more relaxed environment with routine, supervision, and space to settle in comfortably.

Thoughtful Updates

Pet parents stay informed through photos, notes, and clear communication about how the day is going.

Care That Feels Personal

Each booking is approached with attention to temperament, pace, and the routines that help each dog feel safe.

Meet the Team

Jennifer

I grew up with dogs and have always felt most at home in a dog-centered environment. Over the years, I have cared for dogs with different sizes, temperaments, and routines, and that experience has shaped the calm, attentive style behind Pawsome Care. My home is set up to feel structured, safe, and comfortable, with space for both social time and quieter decompression when needed. I personally have two resident dogs at home and also have experience with puppies, senior dogs, and basic training support. Safety and clear communication matter to me, so dogs are separated when I am out, dog park trips are avoided unless specifically requested, and pet parents receive thoughtful daily updates so they always know how their dog is doing.

Located in Davis, CA
