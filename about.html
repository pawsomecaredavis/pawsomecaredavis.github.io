About Us

Personal, home-based care with a calm routine

Pawsome Care is built around small-group, attentive pet care in Davis, CA. The goal is to give dogs a comfortable environment, clear communication with pet parents, and a more personal experience than a large, high-volume facility.

Home-Based Setting

Dogs enjoy a more relaxed environment with routine, supervision, and space to settle in comfortably.

Thoughtful Updates

Pet parents stay informed through photos, notes, and clear communication about how the day is going.

Care That Feels Personal

Each booking is approached with attention to temperament, pace, and the routines that help each dog feel safe.
